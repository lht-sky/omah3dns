Build concept

  • One monolithic body: outer shell plus two vertical and two horizontal partitions form the 9 chambers.
  • Outer-face holders load from outside, as on the v2 cube.
  • Partition holders are back-to-back; they load from the rear before the screwed rear cover (with its own 36 holders) closes the chambers.
  • Plinth carries the "musimorph" logo and houses the driver boards; bottom-row cables drop through a slot in its top.

To confirm with client

  • Shared partitions: one transducer per opening serving both chambers, or two back-to-back? Two typical 12 mm bodies need the partition to grow from 10 to about 26 mm.
  • Real transducer size and pitch (Ø16 / 40 kHz assumed).
  • Print size: 210 mm body fits a 220 mm bed; a smaller printer means splitting into 3 column modules.
